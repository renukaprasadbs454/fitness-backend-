# Legal and product review required

Engineering has not determined legal applicability, lawful basis, statutory roles, or compliance. Obtain qualified Indian privacy counsel and product-owner review before making compliance claims.

## Commencement timeline checked on 2026-11-13

The official commencement notifications phase the Act and Rules. Under the notifications published on 2025-11-13, Rule 4 of the DPDP Rules and Act section 6(9) / section 27(1)(d) commence one year later, on 2026-11-13. Rule 4 concerns registration of Consent Managers; counsel should determine whether FitNerve has that statutory role. The main notice and processing obligations (including Act sections 3-17 and DPDP Rules 3, 5-16, 22 and 23) are scheduled for 2027-05-13, eighteen months after publication. Other provisions commenced on publication. This schedule does not establish FitNerve's applicability or substitute for checking any later Gazette notification.

Official sources:

- [DPDP Act commencement notification, G.S.R. 843(E), 2025-11-13](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/267647.pdf)
- [DPDP Rules, 2025, G.S.R. 846(E), 2025-11-13](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)

Review at least:

- Full notice contents, plain-language presentation, purposes and itemized data, rights and grievance route, contact details, language/accessibility, and version/effective-date process.
- Actual data fiduciary/processor roles, all vendors/subprocessors, storage/transfer regions, contract terms, security safeguards, deletion/backup behavior, and vendor incident obligations.
- Purpose-by-purpose lawful basis and whether each purpose is essential or optional; the seeded purpose catalogue is provisional.
- Retention periods by record category, statutory/business holds, backups, object storage, email, logs, exports, and automated deletion/verification evidence.
- Age eligibility and any child/guardian consent and processing restrictions. No age gate/guardian flow is implemented.
- Nominee eligibility, identity/authority verification, succession/access procedure, and safeguards. Current nominee records are unverified and confer no access.
- Access/correction/deletion identity verification, response timelines, exemptions/holds, human execution, provider propagation, appeals, and evidence. Deletion is not executed by this code.
- Significant Data Fiduciary applicability, additional obligations, DPIA/audit, DPO/contact requirements, and confirm applicability against the commencement timeline above and any later Gazette notification.
- Breach classification, containment, legal assessment, regulator and affected-person notification triggers/deadlines, delivery evidence, and on-call ownership. Current tool only records incidents.
- Review current commencement notifications and rules at deployment; implementation dates and obligations can be staged.

Do not state that FitNerve is fully DPDP-compliant until legal review, operational controls, provider verification, and end-to-end evidence support that conclusion.
